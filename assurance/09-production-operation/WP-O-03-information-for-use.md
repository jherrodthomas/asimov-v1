## 1. Purpose

Information for use, markings and warnings for builders, operators and bystanders: the residual risks of WP-C-05, the exclusions of WP-C-02, the two-person lift, the battery policy, the tether rule, the E-stop, the manual pages upstream has not yet published (GAP-02).

## 2. Manual structure

Builder manual, operator manual, quick start, safety page; what each must contain (SYRS-035).

## 3. Markings and warnings

Pinch points, hot actuators, lifting points, the armed-state indication, the battery compartment.

## 4. Residual risks

Per hazard of WP-C-03 not fully reduced by design or safeguarding.
