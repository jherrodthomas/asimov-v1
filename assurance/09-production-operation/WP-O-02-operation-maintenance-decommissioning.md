## 1. Purpose

Operation, maintenance and decommissioning: the operating envelope limits, the daily and periodic checks, maintenance intervals from the analyses, what a maintenance task may change, battery handling, decommissioning.

## 2. Operation

Operating envelope OE-1 only; the hold point WP-V-07; operator duties; modes.

## 3. Maintenance

Fastener and hip-yoke inspection, harmonic gear replacement interval (FM-04), toe joint, cover embrittlement (FM-26), encoder and IMU checks, battery care.

## 4. Decommissioning

Battery removal and disposal, data wipe, structural parts.
