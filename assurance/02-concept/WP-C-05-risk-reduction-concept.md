## 1. Purpose

Risk reduction concept for the hazards of WP-C-03 by the three-step method of ISO 12100 §6: inherently safe design first (mass, joint travel, guarding, materials), safeguarding and complementary protective measures next (the safety functions of WP-C-04 and the tether and gantry of OE-1), information for use last; with the residual risk per hazard and the adequacy argument.

## 2. Step 1: inherently safe design measures

Mechanical end-stops ≥ 5° outside the software limits (SF-25), chamfered edges and threadlocked fasteners (SF-05), labyrinth guarding at the waist and neck yaw with gaps ≤ 4 mm (SF-07), 7075-T6 and 316L with certificates, a 35 kg mass that cannot be reduced in v1.

## 3. Step 2: safeguarding and complementary protective measures

The safety functions SF-01…SF-04, SF-06, SF-08, SF-11, SF-13…SF-16, SF-20…SF-25 (WP-C-04); the overhead gantry and tether of OE-1 as a complementary measure that carries the fall hazard while no fall-arrest function exists; the E-stop devices; the BMS interlock.

## 4. Step 3: information for use

Two-person lift (SF-18), respiratory PPE when sanding PA12 (SF-17), indoor-only envelope (SF-19), cable management (SF-09), ESD-safe assembly (SF-12), the operator and builder manuals (WP-O-01…WP-O-03).

## 5. Allocation table

Per rated row of WP-C-03: step, measure, residual-risk class; the eighteen rows the concept-phase package left without an adequacy record (GAP-01) are listed first.

## 6. Adequate risk reduction and residual risk

ISO 12100 §5.6 criteria per operating envelope; OE-1 provisionally adequate with the tether, OE-2…OE-4 not.
