## 1. Purpose

Qualification of safety components: certificates, well-tried-component arguments and fault exclusions for the E-stop devices and receiver, the contactors, the BMS, the actuators and their encoders, the thermistors; supplier evidence per WP-M-10.

## 2. Component list

Per element of WP-S-03: part, supplier, certificate or standard, B10d or MTTFD, where used.

## 3. Arguments

Certified component, well-tried component (ISO 13849-2 Annex A–D), or no claim.

## 4. Gaps

Components with no supplier evidence at BL-001: every actuator, the wireless E-stop, the user-supplied BMS.
