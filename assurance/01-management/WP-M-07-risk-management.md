## 1. Purpose

Project risk management plan and register: risks to the assurance path, their owners, responses and review cadence.

## 2. Risk register

R-nn seeded from WP-M-01 §9: upstream never publishes the firmware or the API; the locomotion policy stays unreleased; no fall-protection hardware is bought; one person holds every role; the standards are not available; a learned balance controller pulls the robot into Annex I Part A of the Machinery Regulation.

## 3. Review

At every gate review (WP-M-02 §10) and whenever a decision in the register changes a risk. Liability for powered tests on the lead's own premises is a registered risk (DQ-053 A, 2026-10-21): the existing policy is checked first; hang tests may run under it if machinery under test is not excluded; cover is in place before the first floor test.

## 4. Strategic decisions

Decisions applied from the register are recorded here (§5) once made, with their rationale and the work products they changed.
