## 1. Purpose

Proven-in-use argument evaluation: no proven-in-use claim is made for any element (WP-M-01 T-06); upstream's DIY-kit history is supporting evidence only. This document records what would be needed to make such a claim.

## 2. Candidates

Actuators, the motion control board, the CAN harness, the kernel.

## 3. What a claim would need

Controlled configuration, operating hours, field-problem history per IEC 61508-2 §7.4.10; none exists at BL-001.
