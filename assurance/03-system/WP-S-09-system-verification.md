## 1. Purpose

System verification: that the achieved PL of each safety function meets its PLr (ISO 13849-1 §8) and that the system behaves as specified under the faults of ISO 13849-2; the summary of WP-H-04, WP-V-04 and WP-V-05.

## 2. PL verification

Per safety function: PLr (WP-C-04), category and architecture (WP-S-03), achieved PL (WP-H-04), software evidence (WP-W-06).

## 3. Fault behaviour

Results of the fault-injection campaign against the fault lists.

## 4. Verdict

Per function: verified, verified with conditions, not verified.
