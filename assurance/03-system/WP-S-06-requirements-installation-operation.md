## 1. Purpose

Requirements for installation, operation, maintenance and decommissioning: what the builder, integrator and operator must provide (a tether and gantry in OE-1, a cleared area, a compliant battery, the E-stop within reach), what maintenance the safety functions need (fastener torque, fatigue inspection of the hip yoke, encoder checks), and how a unit is retired.

## 2. Installation and commissioning

Acceptance checks after a DIY build (WP-O-01): material certificates, harness pull test, E-stop test, BMS handshake, watchdog self-test.

## 3. Operation

Operating envelope limits of WP-C-02; the hold point of WP-V-07; operator competence.

## 4. Maintenance

Intervals from WP-H-08 (fatigue) and the DFMEA (harmonic gear, toe joint, covers); what a maintenance task may change and what re-validation it triggers.

## 5. Decommissioning

Battery removal and disposal; data on the boards.
