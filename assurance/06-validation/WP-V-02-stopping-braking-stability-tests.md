## 1. Purpose

Stability, fall and controlled-descent tests: tip-over margin standing and walking, the 50 N lateral load at the shoulder (SYRS-021), stop from each mode, loss of power mid-stride on the gantry, controlled descent from STAND and MOVE, fall energy at contact. The measured controlled-descent time and fall energy feed the contact limits validated in WP-V-03.

## 2. Test specification

ST-nn: mode, load, floor, stop type (E-stop, watchdog, loss of power, commanded descent), tether slack, repetitions, measurement method (motion capture or video, IMU log, force plate).

## 3. Stability tests

ISO 13482 §5.13 as applicable; slope limit; external load.

## 4. Report

Measured times and energies against the model of WP-S-04; the first powered floor test is also the first test under WP-V-07.
