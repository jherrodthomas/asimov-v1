## 1. Purpose

Evaluation of unknown scenarios and field trials: structured trials in OE-1, later OE-2, that look for the triggering conditions WP-C-10 could not list, with the acceptance criteria that would allow a wider operating envelope.

## 2. Trial plan

Hours, scenarios, instrumentation, stop rules; the policy's out-of-distribution monitor as a trigger.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

Demands on each safety function per hour; no uncontrolled fall; no contact above the limits.

## 4. Report

Findings fed back through WP-O-04.
