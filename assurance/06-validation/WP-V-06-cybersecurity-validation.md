## 1. Purpose

Cybersecurity validation and penetration testing of the tele-operation channel, the API, the edge board, the debug ports, the CAN buses and the wireless E-stop link, by a party independent of the implementation.

## 2. Scope and rules of engagement

Per WP-C-07 scenarios; equipment owned by the project; no test with a person in reach of the robot.

## 3. Report

Findings, severity, fixes, re-test.
